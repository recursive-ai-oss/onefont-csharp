## Unicode Character Database (generated tables)

`src/OneFont/Unicode/UnicodeData.g.cs` holds property tables generated by `tools/gen_unicode_tables.py` from the Unicode
Character Database 17.0.0 (Line_Break, East_Asian_Width, General_Category, Grapheme_Cluster_Break, Extended_Pictographic,
Indic_Conjunct_Break, Script, Indic_Syllabic_Category and Indic_Positional_Category). `src/OneFont/Unicode/BidiData.g.cs`
(generated by `tools/gen_bidi_tables.py`) holds the Bidi_Class, Bidi_Paired_Bracket, Bidi_Mirroring_Glyph and Joining_Type data
of the same release. `tests/testdata/unicode/` holds the unmodified conformance files `LineBreakTest.txt`,
`GraphemeBreakTest.txt`, `BidiTest.txt` and `BidiCharacterTest.txt` of that release; only the test project reads them. The

## Noto font subsets (test data only)

The font files in `tests/testdata/fonts/` are subsets of fonts of the Noto project, made with the scripts in `tools/fonts/`:
Noto Sans (Copyright 2015 Google LLC), Noto Sans CJK (Copyright 2014-2021 Adobe), Noto Sans Arabic (Copyright 2015-2020 Google LLC),
Noto Sans Hebrew (Copyright 2019 Google Inc.), and the Noto Sans and Noto Serif script fonts for Balinese, Bengali, Devanagari,
Gujarati, Gurmukhi, Javanese, Kannada, Khmer, Lao, Malayalam, Myanmar, Oriya, Sinhala, Tamil, Telugu, Thai and Tibetan (Copyright 2022
The Noto Project Authors, https://github.com/notofonts/<script>; Bengali Copyright 2025). They keep the glyphs, layout tables and
marks the tests need and have their hinting removed. The fonts are licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1; the license text is
`tests/testdata/fonts/OFL.txt`. They are used only by the test and fuzz projects and are not part of the shipped package.
